How the game works

  • RepAny honest attempt at something on your list. Five minutes counts. Ugly counts. Staring at it and starting counts.
  • MinimumTwo reps a day, like learning the bike: twice a day, sirens or not. Missing one day is allowed. Don’t miss twice.
  • One moreWhen a set ends and you want to stop, that’s the moment. Press +1 for five more minutes.
  • XP1 per minute, +10 for every rep, +5 for every one-more. Levels put plates on that machine’s bar.
  • Season30 days from your first rep. Each week adds one rule. Day 30 is film day, and your outline is already written.
  • No resetThere’s no button to wipe a bad week. Zero days stay visible, and so does everything you did after them.
